About Us

Built for Nigeria.

From Lagos to Kano, Port Harcourt to Abuja — for every corner of the country where someone runs a business and deserves better tools.

The Nigerian Business Problem

Walk into any market across Nigeria — Balogun in Lagos, Wuse in Abuja, Ariaria in Aba, Sabon Gari in Kano — or any salon, pharmacy, or restaurant in the country, and you'll see the same thing: business owners tracking sales in exercise books, calculating profit in their heads, and wondering where their money went at the end of the day.

These aren't small businesses by choice. They're hardworking people running operations that turn over millions of Naira. But they're using tools from 1990 to run a 2026 business. And the "modern" POS solutions? They're either too expensive, too complicated, or they don't work when NEPA takes light.

💡 Why We Built Oja

"Oja" means "market" in Yoruba. And that's exactly who we built this for — the market. The mama who sells provisions at her shop front. The guy running three phone accessory stores across different markets. The restaurant owner who needs to track daily plate sales. The salon owner, the pharmacist, the boutique manager.

We asked a simple question: What if every Nigerian business owner had access to the same quality business tools as a big supermarket — but on their phone, for free, and without needing internet?

That question became Oja POS.

📱 Built for Reality, Not Fantasy

We didn't build Oja in a vacuum. We built it for the reality of Nigerian retail:

Power is unreliable. So Oja works 100% offline. No internet? No light? No problem. Your business doesn't stop.

Data is expensive. So Oja is lightweight. It barely uses any data, and all core features work without a connection.

Naira is king. So everything in Oja is Naira-first. Prices, reports, payroll — all in ₦. No dollar conversions, no confusion.

WhatsApp is everywhere. So we built WhatsApp receipts, WhatsApp credit reminders, WhatsApp low-stock alerts, and a free WhatsApp Storefront right into the app. Share your catalog, take orders — all on WhatsApp, because that's where Nigerian businesses communicate.

🎯 Our Mission

Make every Nigerian business profitable with simple technology.

That's it. Not fancy technology. Not complicated technology. Simple technology. The kind that a business owner can set up in 2 minutes, teach their staff in 5, and rely on every single day. Technology that respects your time, your money, and your hustle.

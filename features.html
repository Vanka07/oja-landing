Offline-First POS

No light? No data? No wahala. Oja POS works completely offline. Record sales, manage inventory, and track profit even without internet. Your data syncs automatically when you're back online.

We built Oja specifically for the Nigerian market — supermarkets, salons, pharmacies, restaurants, and every business in between — where power outages and network issues are a daily reality. Your business doesn't stop just because NEPA takes light.
